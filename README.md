# 📊 2023 Sales Performance Dashboard – Power BI Project

## 🔍 Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes **company-wide sales performance for 2023**, focusing on the key metrics that drive decision-making — **Total Revenue, Total Profit, Quantity Sold, and Profit Margin**.  

The goal is to uncover which **regions** and **products** generate the most value, highlight areas of improvement, and visualize the company’s sales journey throughout the year.

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- 💰 **Total Revenue:** 60.02M  
- 📈 **Total Profit:** 16.6M  
- 📦 **Total Quantity Sold:** 7,430 units  
- 📊 **Average Profit Margin:** 0.28  

### Regional Performance
- **Central** and **North** regions lead in total revenue (each contributing around 20%+).  
- **East Region** achieved the **highest average profit margin (~0.28)**, showing efficient operations.  
- **South and West** regions performed solidly but with slightly lower margins.

### Product Insights
- **Phones and Laptops** are the top revenue drivers (each exceeding 6.7M).  
- **Mouse, Monitor, and Tablet** also performed consistently across regions.  
- Products like **Headphones and Printers** show lower but stable sales, suggesting a mature market.

### Trend Highlights
- **March** and **November** stand out as peak sales months.  
- Revenue fluctuations suggest seasonality in demand — potentially linked to promotions or holiday cycles.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX measures, visualization design  
- **Excel / CSV Dataset** – Source for all transactional sales data  
- **DAX Calculations** – Profit Margin = Total Profit ÷ Total Revenue  
- **Slicers** – Region & Product filters for dynamic analysis  
- **KPIs** – Cards for Revenue, Profit, Quantity, and Margin
